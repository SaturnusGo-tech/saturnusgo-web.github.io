01

General

General

This Policy explains what personal data is processed when you use Umbrella Home, why it is needed, who it may be shared with, how long it is kept, and what rights users have.

The Policy applies to Customers, Hosts, and Workers, as well as Umbrella Support requests. Internal Admin and Support staff access data only to the extent required to perform their duties.

Processing is governed by the Argentine Constitution, Ley 25.326 de Protección de los Datos Personales, Decreto 1558/2001, AAIP rules, and applicable consumer protection law.

04

Sources

How data enters Umbrella

Most data is provided directly by the user during registration, order creation, communication, payment, or use of Aurora.

Some data is created automatically, including order status, execution events, payment results, diagnostic records, IP, push token, and technical identifiers.

Worker performance data is created when a task is accepted, arrival is recorded, a proof is added, an expense is registered, or mandatory actions are completed.

Guest, recipient, or contact-person data is usually provided by the Host or Customer. Umbrella Home uses it only for invitation, access, contact for a specific order, handoff, safety, support, and lawful records.

05

Purposes

How data is used

Data is used for account registration and protection; price calculation; order creation and confirmation; Worker assignment; route building; performance of Cleaning, Laundry, and Assignments; payment and refund; Host automations; chat and support; Aurora; receipts; fraud prevention; dispute resolution; and tax or other legal requirements.

Data is not used for purposes incompatible with the purposes for which it was collected.

Umbrella Home does not sell personal data and does not share it with ad networks for behavioral advertising.

06

Legal bases

Legal bases

Data needed to create and perform an order is processed within contractual relationships under article 5.2.d of Ley 25.326.

Separate consent is used for optional permissions such as microphone, camera, photo library, background geolocation, translations, marketing messages, and international transfers where legally required.

Separate consent is also required for automatic off-session charges under a Host schedule. It is recorded with amount, frequency, payment method, and automation version.

An iOS system permission is not a legal notice by itself. When a feature is optional or context-sensitive, Umbrella shows the purpose, recipients, consequences of refusal, and withdrawal method before or at collection.

07

Choice

Required and optional data

Phone number and core order parameters are required to use the service. An address is required for an order involving a visit or delivery. A purchase assignment cannot start without an agreed budget.

Camera, microphone, photos, notifications, Aurora, and translation are optional unless the user chooses the relevant feature.

If Customer declines geolocation, Customer must be able to enter an address manually as required by Apple policy.

08

Sharing

Data sharing between Customer and Worker

An assigned Worker receives the Customer name, operational contact number, address, time, and instructions needed for the specific order.

Customer may see the Worker name, permitted contact data, execution status, and current geolocation during an active task.

After completion, cancellation, or reassignment, access is limited. The previous Worker should not retain access to current Customer data.

09

Host

Host

Host provides profile data, properties, units, templates, schedules, automations, and access instructions.

Umbrella verifies property ownership and Host role on the backend. Another Host cannot access a property only by changing an identifier.

Access instructions are data with increased operational risk. Access and administrative opening of such instructions must be limited and audited.

For guest and recipient data, the Host is responsible for lawful first collection and minimum notice to the guest. Umbrella is the controller for in-product processing, invitations, access, support, safety, and orders created through Umbrella Home.

10

Third parties

Guests, recipients, and contact people

Umbrella Home may store a guest stay, invitation status, guest name, masked phone, phone hash, access dates, guest count, notes, route contacts, and handoff instructions.

The first message to a guest or recipient explains that the contact is used for Umbrella Home, who provided the data, how to contact the operator, and how to request correction or deletion when the data is not needed for an active order, safety, dispute, or law.

A Worker receives guest, recipient, or contact-person data only to the extent needed for the specific task. Use outside the order, repeat contact, or independent marketing is prohibited.

If an adult user provides data about a minor guest, Umbrella processes it only as operational stay or order data and does not create a child behavioral or advertising profile.

11

Payments

Payments

Stripe processes bank card data, payment identifiers, device data, IP, transaction address, and antifraud signals.

Stripe may act as a processor on behalf of Umbrella and as an independent controller for fraud prevention, authentication, and financial compliance.

Umbrella stores only tokenized identifiers, limited card characteristics, amounts, authorization statuses, capture, release, refund, dispute, fiscal document, and risk data. Umbrella does not store the full card number or CVC.

Stripe Privacy Center
12

Aurora

Aurora and artificial intelligence

Aurora processes text, voice transcript, draft structure, checklist, item images, and support context.

An uploaded voice file is sent for transcription and is not stored by Umbrella as a permanent audio recording. The transcript, Aurora responses, language, session metadata, and technical audio hash are retained.

When automatic translation is used, message text is sent to an AI provider. Automatic translation is off by default, and the original text remains available.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train models by default. Actual retention depends on the endpoint and account settings: Responses generally has 30 days of abuse monitoring and may store application state when store=true, Realtime and transcription sessions have their own rules, and Zero Data Retention requires separate approval.

Aurora does not make final decisions about refunds, payouts, disputes, cancellation of started orders, Worker blocks, or other material user rights.

Responses API
structured Aurora responses, support, and image analysis; application state is stored only when storage is enabled or a mode requires it
Realtime
voice dialog and streaming responses; used under OpenAI's separate retention policy for realtime endpoints
Transcription
speech-to-text conversion; Umbrella keeps the working transcript, not a permanent audio recording
OpenAI API Data Controls
13

Automation

Matching, dispatch, antifraud, and automated processing

Umbrella may use automated rules for price calculation, order completeness checks, Worker matching, routing, fraud/Radar signals, support priority, translations, and execution statuses.

This processing helps perform the order, but it does not replace a human where a decision materially affects user rights: final refunds, disputes, account blocking, or Worker access to work require human review or an available appeal.

14

Location

Geolocation

Customer may use geolocation to choose an address or may enter an address manually.

Worker background geolocation is used for matching, arrival, active performance, safety, and permitted administrative operations.

The last Worker coordinate is kept for up to one hour. Customer receives only the current point of an active task, not older than 120 seconds. Tracking sessions are kept for 90 days, and operational audit events for 365 days.

Umbrella does not create a hidden permanent route history for Worker movement outside work context.

15

Files

Photos and files

Photos and files may be used for laundry recognition, proofs, receipts, profile, Host properties, and support.

They may be available to participants of the relevant order and authorized Support. Files are not publicly published to an undefined audience.

Hidden image metadata is removed or not used when it is not needed for evidence, security, or performance.

17

Transfers

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Argentina, including in the United States and other jurisdictions.

International transfers are made only when a permitted basis applies: data processing agreement, AAIP standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, an adequate-protection country, the data subject's express consent, or another exception allowed by article 12 of Ley 25.326.

If no permitted transfer mechanism is available for a provider or region, the relevant feature is not used for personal data until that mechanism is available.

AAIP international transfers
18

Retention

Retention periods

Retention depends on the data category, order status, tax obligations, claims, safety, and technical deletion from active systems. Data is deleted, restricted, or irreversibly de-identified when the processing purpose and mandatory periods no longer require identification.

Account and profile
kept until account deletion, deactivation, or pseudonymization, except data that must be kept for law, dispute, payment, or safety
Drafts and voice transcripts without order
kept only while needed to continue the flow, support, safety, or investigate an error; then deleted or de-identified by the retention process
Orders, chats, proofs, support, and handoff summaries
kept for the period needed for contract performance, warranties, claims, fiscal records, safety, and protection of parties' rights
Fiscal documents and payment records
kept in the mandatory tax, contractual, financial, and antifraud scope; public document identifiers are not deleted where law requires retention
Last Worker coordinate

19

Deletion

Account deletion

The user may initiate account deletion directly in the app. Simple deactivation is not deletion, but it may be used as the first technical step if immediate physical deletion is blocked by law, payment, fiscal documents, disputes, or a related record.

The deletion lifecycle includes ending active sessions, deleting avatars and optional profile data, deleting one-time codes, withdrawing optional consents, restricting processing, and irreversibly pseudonymizing records that must remain for tax, payment, contractual, dispute, or security purposes.

If a legally related record blocks immediate physical deletion, Umbrella does not promise to remove the entire identifying record set at the moment of request. The user receives confirmation of the result: what was deleted, what was pseudonymized, what was retained, and why.

Apple requires account and associated user data deletion from inside the app, instead of sending the user to ordinary support.

Apple account deletion requirements
20

Security

Security

Umbrella applies role separation, authentication, TLS, session protection, audit of critical operations, backend ownership checks, limited storage access, and incident response procedures.

Raw transcripts, addresses, coordinates, access instructions, full payment data, and secrets are excluded from ordinary technical logs when they are not needed for security or error investigation.

For a security incident, Umbrella assesses user risk, limits further access, preserves necessary evidence, notifies affected users and authorities when required by law or applicable platform rules.

Security measures take Resolución AAIP 47/2018 recommendations into account.

Resolución AAIP 47/2018
21

Rights

User rights

The user may request information, access, correction, update, blocking, confidentiality, or deletion of data.

An access request is reviewed within 10 calendar days. Correction, update, or deletion is completed within 5 business days. Access is free at least once every six months unless a separate legitimate interest exists.

Requests should be sent to mercury@saturnusgo.com. The Operator may perform proportionate identity verification without collecting excessive documents.

If Umbrella satisfies a correction or deletion request for data already sent to a recipient or processor, Umbrella notifies that recipient within 5 business days where applicable under Ley 25.326.

If the response is unsatisfactory, the user may contact AAIP or use the habeas data procedure.

AAIP
22

Consent

Withdrawal of consent

The user may disable optional permissions in iOS and Umbrella settings, turn off translation, notifications, or automation, and opt out of marketing.

Withdrawal applies going forward and does not make processing already performed under valid consent or contract unlawful.

If withdrawal makes an active order impossible to perform, Umbrella explains the consequences before stopping processing.

23

Sensitive data

Sensitive data

Umbrella does not require race or ethnic origin, political opinions, religion, trade union membership, health information, or sex life information.

Intentional collection of this data is prohibited except for a strictly described lawful exception. Such data should not be included in chat, voice, or instructions.

If sensitive data is accidentally provided, Umbrella limits visibility, does not use it for profiling, and deletes or redacts it when it is not needed for safety, a legal obligation, dispute, or protection of vital interests.

24

Age

Age

Umbrella accounts are intended for people over 18. The product is not directed to children and does not create child advertising or behavioral profiles.

Minor guest data may appear only through an adult Customer or Host for a stay, access, or a specific service. Such data is used in a minimum operational scope.

25

Analytics

Marketing and analytics

Operational messages about authorization, order, Worker, payment, security, and contract changes are not marketing.

Marketing messages require a separate opt-in opportunity and a simple free opt-out.

Technical analytics are used for error diagnosis, performance, and security. Data is aggregated or de-identified where possible.

26

Changes

Changes to this Policy

Changes are published with a new date and version. Material changes to purposes, recipients, or data scope are communicated in advance.

A new Policy cannot retroactively legalize incompatible processing. When new consent is required, Umbrella requests it separately.
