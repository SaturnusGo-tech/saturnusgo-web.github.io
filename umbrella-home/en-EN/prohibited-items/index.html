01

Technical classification

Dangerous Goods

Umbrella Home is not a licensed dangerous-goods carrier. All dangerous-goods classes requiring special transport conditions, labelling, packaging, documents or authorization are prohibited in a standard order.

How this list is applied

  • This list is not exhaustive. If Argentine law, a provincial rule, a regulator, a licence or safety conditions require special authorization, the item is not accepted in a standard Umbrella Home order.
  • An Umbrella Home service provider may refuse an item when packaging is damaged, contents are undisclosed, labelling is inconsistent, there is odour, leakage, pressure, tampering or risk to people, property or the environment.
  • If an item is only lawful through a licensed carrier, pharmacy, sanitary transport, weapons regime, controlled chain or age-gated sale, the order must be cancelled or redirected into a separate authorized process.
