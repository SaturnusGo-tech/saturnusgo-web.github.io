Umbrella Home / Mar del Plata

The home is ready, without turning your day into emergencies.

Umbrella Home is a mobile service for hosts and temporary rental managers. It helps coordinate cleaning, laundry, shopping, restocking, apartment preparation, and simple requests around each stay.

Join the pilotSee how it works

For hosts

A good temporary rental is almost invisible when everything works. It becomes valuable when nothing breaks between one guest and the next.

The digital product is ready. We are preparing the first local pilot in Mar del Plata with operators who understand the city, real timing, and the level of care a guest expects.

What it organizes

Home tasks, gathered into one operation.

This is not a gallery of disconnected services. It is a clear way to request, track, and confirm what needs to happen before the apartment is ready again.

Cleaning

Coordination for regular, deep, or post-stay cleaning.

Laundry

Pickup, tracking, and return of linens or garments according to the host operation.

Shopping

Simple supermarket, pharmacy, or home-stay supply requests.

Restocking

Paper, soap, water, coffee, amenities, and basic items defined by each property.

Preparation

A visual checklist before arrival: order, essentials, condition, and confirmation.

Simple requests

Small operational requests during a stay without turning every message into an emergency.

How it would work

Fewer scattered chats. More clarity before, during, and after.

The host creates a request, adds instructions, and sees progress. During the pilot, each local capability is validated before availability opens.

01

The host defines the task

What needs to happen, when, at which property, and with which instructions.

02

The operation is coordinated

Umbrella Home organizes the request and connects it to the available process for that task type.

03

The result is confirmed

The task closes with status, visual proof when needed, and a clear result for the host.

Trust

Aesthetic matters. Traceability matters more.

The goal of the pilot is not to promise infinite coverage. It is to prove that home operations can feel clear, careful, and verifiable.

Control

Every request should have status, operational ownership, and an understandable close.

Care

The experience is designed for real properties, not a generic demo.

Evidence

When a task is done, the host needs to know what changed and what is ready.

Honest pilot

Local capability is validated first. Scaling comes after.

First market

Mar del Plata first, with hosts who live the operation for real.

We are speaking with temporary rental managers, hosts, and operators to tune the pilot before opening it publicly.

If you manage apartments or work with temporary stays, your experience can help the product start with local judgment.

Next step

If this feels useful, let’s talk before the pilot.

You do not need to buy anything now. We want to understand how you work, what repeats every day, and where Umbrella Home can remove real friction.
