Personal trial terms

These rules apply to Falcon's personal seven-day trial workspace. Company access is granted separately by the company administrator.

falcon@saturnusgo.com ↗

1. One person, seven days

A personal workspace is for one human. The period starts when Falcon successfully creates the workspace after required email verification, including verification after Google signup. Signing in again, another device, reinstalling the app or adding a sign-in method to the same verified account does not start another week. Do not share the account with other people.

2. After the trial

Viewing and export remain available. Creating, changing data and executing tests in the personal workspace are blocked. The end of seven days does not itself trigger automatic workspace deletion. Storage and data requests are described in the privacy policy.

3. Payments

This trial does not collect payment details or initiate charges. Payments and checkout are currently unavailable. There is no automatic paid subscription renewal.

4. Company invitations

A company invitation is a separate grant of access with that company's existing roles, projects and sign-in checks. Ending the personal trial does not end independently granted company access. The trial itself does not create company membership or move personal data into a company.

5. Authorized use and contact

Use Falcon only for sites, applications and content you are authorized to test and handle. Provide only data you have authority to share. For trial and access questions, contact falcon@saturnusgo.com.
