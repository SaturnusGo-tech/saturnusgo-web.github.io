Falcon privacy policy

This policy describes data handling for the website, Falcon personal accounts, company workspaces and demo requests.

falcon@saturnusgo.com ↗

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Mercury Rucks, an individual developing Falcon, in Argentina. Correspondence address: Salta 420D, Argentina. Falcon is a project name, not a separate legal entity.

For privacy questions and requests: falcon@saturnusgo.com. Project notifications are sent from mercury@saturnusgo.com.

2. Falcon accounts and authentication

Falcon processes your email, account identifiers and profile details supplied by you or your company administrator, such as your name, optional contact details and avatar. Email codes confirm mailbox access. Personal workspace creation records the current trial-terms version and creation time.

Protected session cookies, authentication status and server audit records support sign-in, access checks, abuse prevention and investigation of service issues. Technical records can include connection details, IP addresses, URLs, browser or device information and email delivery status. A browser tab may keep a short-lived return path. Desktop uses separate workspace sessions and a short-lived system-browser handoff.

3. Google and corporate SAML sign-in

Google sign-in requests only openid, email and profile. These scopes identify your Google account and may provide basic profile information; Falcon uses the provider identifier and verified email for sign-in and explicit account linking. This sign-in request does not request Gmail, Drive or Calendar access. A matching email alone does not grant access to a company.

Corporate SAML processes authentication assertions, the configured stable subject and authentication metadata from the company's identity provider. The company administrator configures the provider, allowed employee mappings and required verification. Linking a provider to an existing company account requires the current company account's sign-in checks.

4. Workspace content and permissions

Falcon processes the projects, cases, steps, plans, runs, results, evidence, attachments and integration settings you or authorized company users provide. Memberships, roles and company policy determine access to company workspaces. Company administrators can manage company users and access settings.

If you choose a connected integration, the data needed for that action is exchanged with that service. Use only sites and content you are authorized to test or share. Questions about company-controlled access or content may also require your company administrator.

5. Service providers for accounts

Cloudflare serves and protects the web service. Railway hosts the API and database. The configured SMTP service delivers account verification codes and access emails. Google or your company's SAML identity provider processes the sign-in you choose under its own service terms and privacy information.

Providers receive service content and technical, authentication or delivery information needed for their role. Processing may occur outside your country. Contact falcon@saturnusgo.com for questions about the configured services and available information on data handling.

6. Account storage, trial expiry and requests

The seven-day personal trial is an access period, not an automatic deletion deadline. After it ends, viewing and export remain available while creation, changes and test execution are blocked in the personal workspace. Account, workspace, session, audit and provider records have different operational and deletion cycles; this notice does not set a fixed retention period for all account data.

For access, correction, export, deletion or privacy questions, write to falcon@saturnusgo.com. Requests may require verification of your identity and authority over the relevant workspace. The existing website and demo-request processing is described separately below.

7. Website and demo: What is collected

The demo form asks for your own email address and your consent. The request stores your email, selected language, request and consent timestamps, consent-text version, expiry and technical notification status, including delivery attempts. You can browse without submitting the form.

Hosting and email providers receive technical connection or delivery data needed for their services, which can include IP addresses, URLs, message content and delivery status. Email correspondence includes the sender, message and information you choose to provide. Please do not send unnecessary passwords, identity documents or payment details.

8. Website and demo: Purpose and consent

Your email is used to contact you about a Falcon demo and arrange a meeting. A notification is sent to the project owner so they can respond. Submitting the form does not book a calendar slot, trigger an automatic calendar invitation or subscribe you to marketing.

Processing the demo request is based on your voluntary consent through an initially unchecked checkbox; where GDPR applies, Article 6(1)(a). Without this consent, the form cannot send the request. Technical delivery, abuse prevention and consent records support secure operation; where applicable, these rely on legitimate interests, balanced against your rights. Requests required by law are handled on the relevant legal basis.

9. Website and demo: Providers and international processing

Cloudflare serves and protects the website. Railway hosts the API and database. Resend delivers the owner notification. Proton Mail (Proton AG, Switzerland) hosts falcon@saturnusgo.com for correspondence. Yahoo receives the notification sent to the project owner. These providers receive the information needed for their services, under their applicable terms. The controller in Argentina can access requests and correspondence. Data is not sold.

Provider infrastructure and email delivery may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Locations and protection rules can differ. You may ask the contact address for information about applicable arrangements and safeguards. Consent to a demo does not replace legal requirements for international transfers. Data may also be disclosed when a binding legal requirement applies.

10. Website and demo: Retention

Demo requests, consent records and associated notification metadata are kept in the active database for at most six months from submission. Expired requests are excluded from pending notifications and periodically deleted. Data may be deleted earlier when no longer needed or when a valid deletion or withdrawal request is received.

Email correspondence is kept as needed to handle your enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations or claims. Provider logs, delivered emails and backups have separate retention and deletion cycles; removal from the active database does not immediately remove these copies. Ask the contact address for details.

11. Website and demo: Withdrawal and your rights

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing falcon@saturnusgo.com, without an account or a reason. Withdrawal stops future processing based on that consent and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. A notification already delivered to the email provider may still arrive.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy, object to processing, and complain to a competent authority or court. In Argentina, the supervisory authority is the AAIP. Write from the submitted address where possible; proportionate information may be requested to verify identity. Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits.

12. Website and demo: Security

Access to requests is limited to operating the service and responding to you. Technical safeguards reduce risks but cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not include sensitive information in a demo request.

13. Website and demo: Minors

Do not submit the form if applicable law does not allow you to give valid consent independently. Contact the address above if information was supplied without the necessary consent.

14. Website and demo: Changes

The current policy is published here with its update date. A new publication does not itself authorize new purposes. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. The current account policy is published in English and Russian. Older demo translations are labeled separately.
