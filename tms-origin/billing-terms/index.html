2. Price protection for an uninterrupted subscription

Your Protected Rate is the recurring subscription fee agreed in the order for the same continuously active subscription, plan and billing interval. For a subscription already active when catalog prices change, the Protected Rate is the recurring rate agreed for that subscription. Any separately disclosed one-time discount or introductory period follows the terms accepted in the order.

The Protected Rate applies to each renewal while the subscription continues on those terms. Changes to Falcon’s catalog prices or standard terms do not increase the Protected Rate. A change of payment details or account contact details does not affect it. A temporary payment failure does not affect it while the subscription remains in effect, including while payment is past due during an applicable recovery or grace period. The Protected Rate applies to the recurring base subscription fee. Applicable taxes, statutory charges and separately ordered seats, subscriptions or services are governed by their disclosed terms.
