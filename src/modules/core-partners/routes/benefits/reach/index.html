Reach model

The best partner placement feels like an answer.

People do not need more random offers. They need a helpful suggestion near the moment they are already solving: where to go, what to order, how to move, and what to keep for later.

Demand

Where partner reach becomes useful.

NearbyLocal discovery with a reason.Restaurants, cafés, wellness spaces, and venues can appear when the user is browsing places around them.→
JourneyMovement creates natural context.A user can find a place, request a ride there, save it, and return later without switching systems.→
RepeatSaved intent is quieter and stronger.Collections let partner demand survive beyond one session and become part of a future city plan.→
ServiceDelivery reaches users at home or on the move.Useful service partners can meet people through order, pickup, or delivery moments instead of generic promotion.→

Goal

Fewer impressions. Better timing.

The partner branch should sell relevance and trust, not inflated numbers. That is why the page is intentionally calm.
