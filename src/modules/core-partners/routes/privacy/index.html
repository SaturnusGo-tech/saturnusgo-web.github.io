Privacy Policy

How SaturnusGo handles data across the app, landing pages, waitlist, partner intake, and launch operations.

Last updated: 01 Sep 2025

Information we collectHow we use informationPartners and providersHow long we keep dataYour rightsSecurity and safeguardsChildren
01

Data

Information we collect

SaturnusGo collects only the information needed to operate the product, improve reliability, support users, and manage partner workflows.

Account data
Name, email, phone, language, settings, authentication records, and support context.
Product activity
Ride intent, delivery requests, places viewed or saved, route actions, waitlist entries, and app usage events.
Location data
Approximate or precise location may be used for pickup, routing, nearby places, and transport features when permission is granted.
Payments
Payment tokens, transaction metadata, receipts, and payment status. We do not store full card numbers.
02

Purpose

How we use information

  • Operate rides, delivery, places, transport, saved collections, support, and partner surfaces.
  • Improve reliability, performance, safety, fraud prevention, and product quality.
  • Send transactional messages, service updates, and launch communication where permitted.
  • Understand product demand and prepare launch operations without selling personal data.
03

Sharing

Partners and providers

We share limited information only when needed to provide the requested service, process payments, support operations, comply with law, or protect users and the platform.

Service partners
A partner may receive the data required to fulfill a ride, delivery, place request, booking, or support case.
Infrastructure
Cloud, analytics, payment, security, and support providers process data under contractual safeguards.
Legal requests
We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.
04

Retention

How long we keep data

We keep data for as long as needed for product operation, support, fraud prevention, legal requirements, and legitimate business records. When data is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

05

Control

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Some requests may be limited by legal or operational obligations.

06

Security

Security and safeguards

We use technical and organizational measures to protect data, including access controls, provider review, monitoring, and limited internal access based on operational need.

07

Age

Children

SaturnusGo is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us.
