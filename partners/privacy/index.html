This Privacy Policy explains how SaturnusGo (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our applications, websites, and services (the “Services”).

#Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account Data: name, email, phone, password hashes.
  • Profile & Booking Data: preferences, itineraries, hotels/events, loyalty details.
  • Payments: tokenized payment identifiers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Device & Usage: app version, device IDs, crash logs, cookies.
  • Location (with consent): approximate or precise to provide mobility features.

#How we use information

We process data to:

  • Provide and improve the Services; personalize content and recommendations.
  • Enable bookings, payments, receipts, and customer support.
  • Prevent fraud, ensure trust & safety, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Communicate updates, transactional messages, and—where permitted—marketing.

#Legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligations.

#Sharing & disclosure

We may share information with:

  • Hospitality/venue partners to fulfill bookings and event access.
  • Payment processors and identity verification providers.
  • Service providers under contract (e.g., cloud, analytics, support).
  • Law enforcement or regulators where required by law.

#Data retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and meet legal requirements. We apply minimization wherever feasible.

#International transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., SCCs or equivalent).

#Your rights

Subject to your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your personal data.

#Children

Our Services are not directed to children under the applicable age of consent. We delete data if we learn of such collection.

#Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, and loss.

#Contact

For privacy inquiries or requests: privacy@saturnusgo.com.
