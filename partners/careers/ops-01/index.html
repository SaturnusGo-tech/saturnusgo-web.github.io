COO / Operations Director

You will turn the product promise into operational reality. This role is for someone who can create structure without killing speed: partner workflows, launch playbooks, issue ownership, and service standards.

Role brief

What this role is really about.

Shape the operating system for partners, support, city launches, escalation paths, and the practical reality behind the product.

01What you will own

Design partner onboarding, review, and escalation workflows. Build launch playbooks for city services, delivery scenarios, and support operations. Create clear ownership across product, support, legal, compliance, and partners.

02What strong looks like

Partner and support workflows have owners, SLAs, and escalation paths. Launch playbooks are practical enough for real city operations. The company has fewer unclear handoffs.

03Signals we care about

Experience building operational systems in marketplace, mobility, travel, or service products. Strong judgment under ambiguity and pressure. Ability to make processes readable, enforceable, and lightweight.

How we hire

A short process with high signal.

  1. 01Founder screen: role fit, timing, and product judgment.
  2. 02Role deep dive: your strongest work and the decisions behind it.
  3. 03Focused practical task or portfolio review, depending on the role.
  4. 04Final alignment on ownership, availability, and first 30 days.
