QA Engineer

You will protect the user experience before public launch. The work is not just finding bugs; it is helping the team understand risk, reproduce issues quickly, and release with fewer surprises.

This role is part of the first SaturnusGo build team. The brief is intentionally practical: what you own, what good looks like, and how we decide fit.

Role brief

What this role is really about.

Turn product quality into a system: regression coverage, release checks, precise bug reports, and confidence around core journeys.

01What you will own

Map critical user journeys and maintain focused regression coverage. Write clear reproduction steps, severity notes, and release-risk summaries. Collaborate with product and engineering to define what must never break.

02What strong looks like

Critical user journeys have clear regression coverage. Release risk is visible before it reaches users. Bug reports are precise enough for fast product and engineering decisions.

03Signals we care about

Experience testing web or mobile products with practical QA discipline. Ability to distinguish cosmetic issues from product-breaking risks. Clear written communication and strong attention to edge cases.

How we hire

A short process with high signal.

  1. 01Founder screen: role fit, timing, and product judgment.
  2. 02Role deep dive: your strongest work and the decisions behind it.
  3. 03Focused practical task or portfolio review, depending on the role.
  4. 04Final alignment on ownership, availability, and first 30 days.
