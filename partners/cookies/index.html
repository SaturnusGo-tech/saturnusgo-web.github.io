Cookie Policy

A clear explanation of the browser and device technologies used by SaturnusGo websites and product surfaces.

Last updated: 01 Sep 2025

What cookies areHow SaturnusGo uses cookiesManaging cookiesMobile identifiersPolicy updates
01

Basics

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies help a website remember a device, keep a session stable, store preferences, and understand whether the product is working correctly.

02

Use

How SaturnusGo uses cookies

Essential
Required for security, session continuity, language, and core website behavior.
Preferences
Remember choices such as language and interface settings.
Analytics
Help us understand performance, page usage, waitlist conversion, and product interest.
Marketing
May be used for campaign measurement or relevant product communication where permitted.
03

Control

Managing cookies

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Some features may work less reliably when essential cookies are disabled.
  • Where available, SaturnusGo consent controls can be used to adjust non-essential categories.
04

Mobile

Mobile identifiers

Mobile platforms may use device identifiers or permission-based signals for analytics and product quality. You can manage many of these controls in your operating system settings.

05

Updates

Policy updates

This policy may change as SaturnusGo adds product surfaces, launch regions, analytics controls, or partner workflows.
