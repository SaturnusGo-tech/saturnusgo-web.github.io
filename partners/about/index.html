Partners / About

A partner system for real city decisions.

SaturnusGo is built around four product modules: rides, delivery, places, and transport. The partner program helps useful local businesses appear inside those decisions with less noise and more context.

What matters

The user should understand the next useful action immediately.

A good partner integration is calm, practical, and easy to act on. It should answer where to go, how to get there, what to order, or why this place deserves attention now.

Principles

How we decide what belongs in the partner layer.

ContextRelevant before visible.A partner surface appears when it helps the current city task, not just because there is space on the page.→
ActionEvery placement needs a clear next step.Book, route, save, request, order, or contact. If there is no action, the surface is not ready.→
QualityThe product should feel curated.We prefer fewer, better partner moments over noisy lists that make the app feel like an ad board.→

Positioning

Useful partners become part of the route, not decoration around it.

That is the standard for the whole partner branch: simple language, clear structure, and visual calm.
